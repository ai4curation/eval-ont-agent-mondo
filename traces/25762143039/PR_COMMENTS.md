This PR successfully implements **Phase 1 (Template 1)** of the VeNom integration plan, adding cross-references and metadata for 229 active VeNom diagnosis codes to existing Mondo non-human animal (NHA) disease terms.

## Changes Made

### VeNom Xref Integration (Template 1 - Complete)
- **229 unique VeNom codes** added as `xref` annotations to **209 unique Mondo NHA terms**
