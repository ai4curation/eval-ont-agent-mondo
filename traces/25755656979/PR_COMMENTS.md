## Summary

This PR adds a new term **preneoplastic lesion** (MONDO:7770012) as requested in issue #9781.

### Changes
- Added new term `MONDO:7770012` with name **preneoplastic lesion**
- Parent: `MONDO:0021074` (precancerous condition)
- Definition: "A precancerous condition characterized by accumulation of some molecular alterations necessary for malignant transformation in a clonal proliferation of cells, representing an intermediate stage in carcinogenesis with increased risk of progression to invasive neoplasia."
- Definition references: PMID:37775701, PMID:39754221, PMID:40624726, PMID:40684183
- Added term tracker item linking back to issue #9781

### Rationale
During discussion in issue #9781, it was initially proposed that "preneoplastic lesion" be added as an exact synonym of "precancerous condition". However, the requestor correctly clarified that this would be inappropriate because conditions such as "clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential" are precancerous but not preneoplastic lesions. The requestor subsequently confirmed that "preneoplastic lesion" should be created as a **new child term** of `precancerous condition` (MONDO:0021074), rather than of `pre-malignant neoplasm` (MONDO:0000611), since preneoplastic lesions represent a biological stage *before* neoplasm formation.
