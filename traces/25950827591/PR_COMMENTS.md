## Changes Made

### New Term: MONDO:7770012 - SYCE1-related gametogenic failure

**Definition:**
Infertility disorder characterized by gametogenic failure in individuals with mutations in the SYCE1 gene (which encodes a central element protein of the synaptonemal complex required for meiotic chromosome synapsis). The condition presents with sex-dependent phenotypes:
- 46,XX individuals: primary ovarian insufficiency with premature depletion/dysfunction of oocytes
- 46,XY individuals: non-obstructive azoospermia due to meiotic failure and maturation arrest

## Background Research

Research into SYCE1 and gametogenic failure revealed:
- SYCE1 encodes a synaptonemal complex protein essential for meiosis
- Mutations in SYCE1 cause defective chromosome synapsis in both males and females
- Female manifestations: primary ovarian insufficiency/premature ovarian failure (POI/POF)
- Male manifestations: non-obstructive azoospermia (NOA) with meiotic arrest
- Pattern of inheritance: autosomal recessive
- Clinical significance: causative mutations identified in human infertility cases
