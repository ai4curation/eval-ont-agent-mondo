## Response to @sabrinatoro's Question

Hi @sabrinatoro,

To answer your question about "erythropoietic porphyria" vs "erythropoietic protoporphyria":

**They are different conditions:**

1. **Erythropoietic porphyria** (or "congenital erythropoietic porphyria", CEP) - This is MONDO:0009902 (currently labeled as "cutaneous porphyria" in MONDO), also known as Günther disease. It is caused by mutations in the UROS gene and is characterized by severe photosensitivity, mutilating photodermatosis, and hemolytic anemia.

2. **Erythropoietic protoporphyria** (EPP) - This is MONDO:0001676, a separate condition caused by mutations in different genes (FECH for autosomal forms, ALAS2 for X-linked forms).

The term "erythropoietic porphyria" is currently listed as an EXACT synonym for MONDO:0009902 in the ontology. If the ClinGen group's proposed definition for "UROS-related erythropoietic porphyria" refers to congenital erythropoietic porphyria (Günther disease), this would be consistent with the current MONDO:0009902 term.
