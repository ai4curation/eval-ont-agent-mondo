## Implementation Details

### Design Rationale
Following the discussion in issue #9781, the new term "preneoplastic lesion" was created as a child (is_a) of MONDO:0021074 (precancerous condition) rather than as a synonym or as a child of pre-malignant neoplasm. This is the appropriate classification because:

1. **Distinction from synonymy**: The term was not added as a synonym to precancerous condition because not all precancerous conditions are preneoplastic lesions (e.g., clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential).

2. **Relationship to pre-malignant neoplasm**: The term represents lesions that occur *before* neoplasm formation, making it a more specific child of the broader precancerous condition category rather than a sibling of pre-malignant neoplasm.

3. **Definition grounding**: The definition encompasses the core concept of clonal proliferation with accumulated molecular alterations in an intermediate carcinogenic stage, which is well-supported by the provided literature.
