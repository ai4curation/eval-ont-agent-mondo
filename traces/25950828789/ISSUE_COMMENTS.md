## Clarification on @sabrinatoro's Question

Regarding the question about "UROS-related erythropoietic porphyria" vs "erythropoietic protoporphyria":

- **UROS-related erythropoietic porphyria** should map to "Congenital Erythropoietic Porphyria" (CEP), which is currently represented in MONDO as **"cutaneous porphyria"** (MONDO:0009902). This term has "erythropoietic porphyria" as a synonym and includes the relationship to UROS gene mutations.

- **Erythropoietic protoporphyria** (MONDO:0001676) is distinct—it is caused by FECH mutations (or ALAS2 mutations in X-linked cases) and is a separate disease entity.

These are NOT the same condition and should not be conflated.
